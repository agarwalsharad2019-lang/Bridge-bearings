What a bearing does

A bearing is shown here as the interface between a bridge superstructure and its support. The interface must transmit the specified actions while permitting the movement and rotation that the approved design allows.

Selection vocabulary

For each bearing type, the final lesson should state three things:

  1. What action is transferred.
  2. What movement or rotation is allowed.
  3. What evidence controls the selection.

Source-backed wording required: S-01 / S-02 / S-03.

Motion lab

Move the slider to demonstrate a deck translating and rotating as one animated object. The motion is schematic and not to scale.

State: reference position. Enter approved movement data in the final lesson.

Four motion beats

  • Expansion: deck length increases in the permitted direction.
  • Contraction: deck length decreases in the permitted direction.
  • Rotation: the bearing accommodates angular change at the interface.
  • Longitudinal movement: the chosen free/guided direction is made visible.
